Need a toilet in France, fast? Petit Coin shows the public toilets closest to you on a map.

• Nationwide: data from OpenStreetMap, merged with city open data.
• At a glance: opening hours, free or paid, wheelchair access, baby changing.
• Filters: show only toilets that are open now, free, accessible or have baby changing.
• Walking directions: distance and walking time, then one tap to open your maps app.
• English, French and Chinese, following your phone's language.
• Material 3 design with frosted-glass panels, dynamic color or eight preset palettes.

Privacy first: no account, no ad tracking. Your location is used on your phone to find nearby toilets, and anonymous analytics are off by default.
